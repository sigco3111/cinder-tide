## Music

- "An Untold Legend" by SkyleTheFrench, CC0 1.0, from OpenGameArt (https://opengameart.org/content/an-untold-legend). Plays once from the top, then loops its second half.

## Libraries

- Rapier 2D (`@dimforge/rapier2d-compat` 0.21), Apache-2.0, by Dimforge. Rigid bodies for chunks of the world.
- Vite and TypeScript (build tools, not shipped), MIT and Apache-2.0.

## Inspiration (no code or assets used)

- MOLTYN by Hingston Digital: a superhero in a fully simulated pixel world, and its "boil the sea and it rains back down" water cycle.
- Noita by Nolla Games: its GDC 2019 talk on the Falling Everything engine.
- The Powder Toy (GPL-3.0) and Sandspiel (MIT): falling-sand element design and cell layout, as references only.
- Teardown by Tuxedo Labs: readable destruction (disconnected matter always falls).
