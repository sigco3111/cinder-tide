# Cinder Tide: brief (draft)

**The idea.** A superhero on a coastal island where every pixel is real matter, and your powers change the world for good.

**What it's going for.**

- Matter that behaves: heat spreads, water boils, lava meets the sea and sets to stone, and nothing hangs in the air.
- The water cycle as the showpiece: boil the sea, watch the steam become cloud, and the cloud rain it back.
- Powers that combine: a heat beam, tearing rock out and throwing it, freezing, diving shockwaves and a dash, and enemies whose bodies break the way their material does, each with its counter.
- A hero animated in code, with planted feet, a cape and hair that move with the world.

**What it isn't.** A game of sprite sheets or tiles. Everything is simulated and drawn in code.
